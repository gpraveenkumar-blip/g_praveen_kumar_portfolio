# Files to add to /public before deploying

Replace these placeholders with your real files (keep the same filenames, or
update the paths in `lib/data.ts` to match whatever you use):

- `profile-photo.jpg` — square headshot, ≥600×600px
- `resume.pdf` — your resume
- `og-image.jpg` — 1200×630px image used for social share previews
- `favicon.ico`
- `projects/*.jpg` — one cover image + screenshots per project (see `lib/data.ts`)
- `certs/*.jpg` — certificate images
- `testimonials/*.jpg` — headshots for testimonial cards
- `reports/*.pdf` — optional per-project PDF reports referenced in `lib/data.ts`
